Sabah Electricity’s new headquarters, delivered

A RM41.8 million design-and-build fit-out for one of Sabah’s largest GLCs.

In February 2024, Sabah Electricity Sdn Bhd (SESB) announced it would move out of Wisma SESB, its home since 1990, because the ageing building was no longer suitable for occupancy.

JWA was entrusted with the fit-out of the utility’s new headquarters. Completed in 2025 with a contract value of RM41.8 million, it is the largest completed project in JWA’s portfolio to date, delivered by one team from design through construction and handover.

“Throughout the course of the project, JWA Design & Build demonstrated a high level of professionalism, technical competency, and commitment.”

Sabah Electricity Sdn Bhd

The workplace brings together reception, open-plan offices, executive suites and meeting spaces, finished in SESB’s green and blue identity.
