Privacy Notice

How we handle personal data under Malaysia’s Personal Data Protection Act 2010 (PDPA).

What we collect

When you contact us, apply for a role or send an enquiry, we collect the details you give us, such as your name, company, email, phone number, project information and CV.

Why we use it

To respond to enquiries and tender invitations, to prepare proposals, to manage projects with clients, and to consider job and internship applications.

Who we share it with

We do not sell personal data. We share it only with JWA Group companies and service providers who help us deliver the purposes above, or where the law requires it.

How long we keep it

Only as long as needed for these purposes or as required by law, after which it is deleted or anonymised.

Your rights

You may ask to access or correct your personal data, or withdraw consent, by emailing inquiries@jwadesignbuild.com or writing to JWA Design & Build Sdn Bhd, Lot No. 1, 2nd Floor, YLY Plaza, Jalan Tuaran Bypass, Inanam, 88450 Kota Kinabalu, Sabah.
