01About usOur story
Proudly SabahanSince 2010 · Kota Kinabalu

A promise to Sabah,
kept for sixteen years.

2010. A small architectural practice. Today, a contractor. Clients trusted our drawings, then asked us to build them. So we learned to build, and we have not stopped learning since.

Sixteen years on, JWA is a CIDB Grade G7 contractor, trusted by GLCs, government and corporations in Sabah and across Malaysia. Proudly Sabahan in how we hire, how we build, and what we leave behind.
