01About usOur story

Proudly Sabahan, built to an international standard.

JWA began in 2010 as a small architectural practice, run from the back of a living room in Kota Kinabalu. Clients kept asking us to build what we designed. So we learned to build.

Sixteen years later, we are a CIDB Grade G7 contractor trusted by GLCs, government and corporations. The aim has not changed: to raise the standard of building in Sabah, with Sabahan talent.
