Our story

Built on Ambition.
Driven by Progress.

Founded in 2010, JWA began with a vision to raise the standards of Sabah’s built environment.

From our roots in architecture, we have grown into an integrated design and construction group, bringing together multidisciplinary expertise to deliver projects of increasing scale and complexity.

Our ambition goes beyond building projects. We aim to transform how construction is delivered, through excellence, speed, innovation and the development of local talent.

Our founders

Two Founders.
One Vision.

Founded by Datuk Ar. James Wong Kein Peng and Datin Annie Ong Ching Ling, JWA was built on a shared belief that Sabah has the people, potential and capabilities to develop a world-class construction industry.

Their vision continues to shape JWA’s growth: building a company that delivers exceptional projects, nurtures local professionals and continuously challenges itself to achieve more.

“Our goal has never been simply to design buildings. It has always been to build organisations, partnerships and projects that create lasting value for Sabah.”

Founding DirectorDatuk Ar. James Wong Kein Peng

LAM-registered Professional Architect with a Master of Architecture from New Zealand. Founding President of Sabah Young Business & Industry Leaders (SYBIL), and appointed to the DBKK Board of Advisors in 2024.

Founding DirectorDatin Annie Ong Ching Ling

Co-founded JWA in 2010 and leads its operations, keeping every department working as one team.
