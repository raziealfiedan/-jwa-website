Our story

Building Malaysia’s landmarks since 2010.

Founded in Kota Kinabalu as an architectural practice, JWA grew into an integrated CIDB G7 contractor. Clients asked us to build what we designed. Today one team designs, builds and fits out for corporations, GLCs and government across Malaysia.

Our vision

To transform Sabah’s construction industry by being a model of excellence that delivers international standards through an integrated ecosystem of services, while empowering local talent.

Our mission

JWA DESIGN & BUILD SDN BHD is an integrated construction company specializing in commercial and GLC projects. Supported by an in-house multidisciplinary team including project management, architecture, interior design, quantity surveying, procurement, and construction professionals. JWA deliver projects with greater coordination, accountability, and certainty from planning through completion.

Our professional team is dedicated to produce successful projects with integrity and efficient project management that meets regulatory requirements, exceeds client’s expectations and contributes lasting value to both public and private sector developments.
