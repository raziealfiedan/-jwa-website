Our journey

From a living room to a G7 contractor.

Sixteen years, told through the moments that shaped us.

2010

A living room in Kota Kinabalu

Ar. James Wong opens a small architectural practice at the back of a living room, convinced that Sabah can build to any international standard.

2012

The first major project

A large commercial interior teaches the lesson we still work by: clients want quality, not shortcuts.

2014

From design to design and build

Clients keep asking us to build what we design. We become a design and build company, with one team and one contract.

2016

Registered to build

CIDB registration makes JWA a licensed contractor. In the highlands of Kundasang, our golf villas take shape.

2018

Thirty people under one roof

The team grows to 30 architects, designers, engineers, carpenters and project managers. The ibis Styles hotel in Inanam opens.

2020

Ten years, more than 80 projects

A decade in, with more than 80 projects completed and a team that stayed together.

2022

Recognised abroad

Winner at the Asia Pacific Property Awards and ConstructionPlus Asia.

2024

CIDB Grade G7

The highest contractor grade in Malaysia, with no upper limit on project size.

2025

Sabah Electricity headquarters

A RM42 million headquarters for Sabah’s power utility is completed, and SESB names JWA a Top Performer.

2026

The work continues

ConocoPhillips and Sabah Electricity projects are under construction, and 300 local jobs have been created along the way.

Vision, mission and values

To transform Sabah’s construction industry by being a model of excellence that delivers international standards through an integrated ecosystem of services, while empowering local talent.

Why we exist

Sabah deserves buildings of an international standard, built by Sabahans. Every project is a chance to show it can be done.

Our mission

To be an integrated construction company for commercial and GLC projects, with an in-house team of project managers, architects, interior designers, quantity surveyors, procurement and construction professionals, delivering with coordination, accountability and certainty.

Our values

  • Proficiency and quality of design
  • Diversity and teamwork
  • Integrity and reliability
  • Compliance and ethics
  • Professional excellence
