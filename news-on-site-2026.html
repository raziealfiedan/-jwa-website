Projects, September 2026

On site: ConocoPhillips office and Sabah Electricity warehouse

Two major projects worth more than RM54 million are under construction.

JWA currently has two major projects under way.

ConocoPhillips Office (RM23 million). JWA is building the energy company’s new office, designed by SLG, with a target of 2026.

Sabah Electricity Warehouse (RM32 million). A design-and-build warehouse and office facility in Inanam for Sabah Electricity, now in construction.

Together they reflect the scale of work JWA now takes on for multinational and GLC clients, alongside its completed RM42 million headquarters for Sabah Electricity.
