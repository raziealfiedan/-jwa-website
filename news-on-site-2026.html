On site: ConocoPhillips office and SESB warehouse

Two major projects worth over RM54 million are under way.

JWA currently has two major projects under way.

ConocoPhillips Office (RM23 million). JWA is building the energy company’s new office, designed by SLG, with a target of 2026.

Sabah Electricity Warehouse (RM31.88 million). A design-and-build warehouse and office facility in Inanam for Sabah Electricity, now in construction.

Together they reflect the scale of work JWA now takes on for multinational and GLC clients, alongside its completed RM41.8 million headquarters for Sabah Electricity.
