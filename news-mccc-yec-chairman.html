Leading Sabah’s young entrepreneurs

Ar. James Wong elected chairman of MCCC Young Entrepreneurs Committee, Sabah.

JWA founding director Ar. James Wong Kein Peng was elected unopposed as chairman of the Malaysia Chinese Chamber of Commerce Young Entrepreneurs Committee (MCCC-YEC), Sabah Branch, at its annual general meeting.

He set out three focus areas for the committee: building a strong network of young entrepreneurs across Sabah, bringing high-value business education through international speakers, and creating a platform of friendship and trust among business owners.

The role reflects JWA’s commitment to developing Sabahan talent, inside the company and across the wider business community.
