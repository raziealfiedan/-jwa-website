CEO Masterclass Sabah draws 120 entrepreneurs

Organised by MICCI Sabah, with Datuk Ar. James Wong as organising chairman.

The Malaysian International Chamber of Commerce and Industry (MICCI) Sabah Chapter held CEO Masterclass Sabah, bringing together more than 120 young entrepreneurs and business owners to learn from industry leaders.

JWA founding director Datuk Ar. James Wong Kein Peng led the organising committee as organising chairman.

“It’s a night of learning where we can build a powerful network and pair young entrepreneurs with titans of industries to become mentors.”

Ar. James Wong Kein Peng, organising chairman

Speakers shared their business and leadership journeys, and the organisers plan to keep hosting learning and networking events for Sabah’s business community.
