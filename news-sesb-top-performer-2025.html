JWA named Top Performer at Sabah Electricity Vendor Day 2025

Recognised in the Works, Services & Project (Civil Services) category.

JWA Design & Build received the Top Performer Award for Works, Services & Project (Civil Services) at Sabah Electricity Vendor Day 2025, held at the Sabah International Convention Centre (SICC) in Kota Kinabalu on 23 and 24 October 2025.

The two-day programme ran under the theme Empowering Growth Through Sustainable Partnerships. The award recognises vendors whose work and partnership stood out across Sabah Electricity’s operations over the year.

“Your dedication, reliability, and commitment to excellence have contributed significantly to the success of our operations and partnership.”

Certificate citation, Sabah Electricity Vendor Day 2025

The award follows JWA’s delivery of Sabah Electricity’s new headquarters in 2025, a RM41.8 million design-and-build fit-out, and comes as the team continues work on a RM31.88 million warehouse project for the utility.
