Two international awards in 2022

Winner at the Asia Pacific Property Awards and ConstructionPlus Asia.

2022 brought JWA two international wins: Winner at the Asia Pacific Property Awards and Award Winner at ConstructionPlus Asia.

The same year, JWA received the EA 2022 Excellence Award. Its work has also been featured in MIID Decade of Design 11–21, the PAM Sabah Chapter Directory and Property Hunter Magazine, and recognised at the MIID Reka Awards for Design Excellence.

For a Sabah-based, architect-led contractor, the recognition shows local talent delivering to international standards, the heart of JWA’s vision.
